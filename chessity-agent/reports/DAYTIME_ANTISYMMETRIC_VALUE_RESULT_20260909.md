# Perspective-difference trial: average improved, large-error gate failed

The first preparation stopped before training because two roots per unused ECO
group could supply only 18 eligible roots. The separately recorded reservation
repair kept all earlier exclusions and selected 24 roots from ten new groups,
with at most three roots per group. No training or teacher work was repeated.

antisymmetric-value-02 completed 752 updates in 16 epochs and then 11.12 million
requested teacher nodes. Total supervisor time was 24.06 seconds. Selected
development epoch16 at blend0.5 improved broad capped MSE from 96939.56 to
88592.42 cp squared. Negative-correction MAE improved 202.81 to 183.67 cp;
positive-correction MAE improved 281.63 to 271.68 cp. Target MAE was 134.16 cp
versus 212.54; the new-descendant cohort was 148.47 versus 256.64 cp.

Of the 24 new reserved roots, 21 produced eligible independently labelled
descendants. Average error improved 151.31 to 145.80 cp. However, errors of at
least 200 cp increased from six to seven. A C39 position received a +120.13 cp
correction in the wrong direction, enlarging its error from 99.5 to 219.63 cp.
The declared large-error gate failed, so the model was rejected for release.
