# First-boundary quiet mate proof did not qualify

The contact-mate-01 prototype passed all ten correctness tests, including two
independently confirmed Qh3# leaves and their colour mirrors. The 168 warmed,
serial ABBA one-/three-second probes completed without operational errors.

Independent 80k/320k-node review used 400,000 new teacher nodes. Mean finite
three-second regret was identical for parent and prototype: 207.00/209.26cp over
19 finite roots. No new major mistake or forced mate loss appeared, but neither
Bxh7+ root was repaired. The declared tactical gate failed; no matches, release
number, training fit or upload followed. Selected v1.55 remains unchanged.

The detector ran only at qdepth zero. Check evasions and captures inside
quiescence increment that counter, so a quiet mate reached after either step
could still be ignored. A separate trial will remove this scope restriction
while preserving the exact legal mate proof and existing search bounds. This is
a code-derived hypothesis, not a claim that the root failure is already solved.
